KeelTube is creator-owned video with an even keel. It is built around a simple idea: a platform should be able to explain what it did, who decided, and how to appeal it.

AN EVEN KEEL
Stability, due process, and a portable exit belong in the product, not in a support article.

BUILT FOR CREATORS

02  A record you can inspect

03  A real appeal route
    File an appeal with grounds and evidence. An independent reviewer decides, and the outcome is recorded.

WHAT IS DIFFERENT

- Specific notice, not a generic violation message.
- Human review before irreversible harm.
- A hash-chained audit ledger, not a black box.
- Portability is a requirement, not a support ticket.
